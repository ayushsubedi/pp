HR/Payroll/Admin is priced at a generalist/admin rate, not a senior specialist rate. Ranges vary by company tier and negotiation — treat each rate as a midpoint, not a ceiling. The inverter/UPS line assumes a small office (7–9 people); scales up with team/office size.
