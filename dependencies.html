Reading it

  • Nothing in jurisdiction rules, e-sig, or onboarding can really start until its orange-box input lands — those three are owed by Compliance/GTM, not engineering
  • In Scenario C, nothing else starts meaningfully until the CI/CD + IaC + security baseline exists — that's the point of making foundations a required week-1 hire, not an afterthought
  • Transitional Document feeds Merchant Integration directly (gap-driven recommendations) — it should be built slightly ahead, not in lockstep
  • QA and the pen test gate go/no-go together with onboarding being ready — both have to be true, not either

Scenario B has the same shape, minus the dedicated Foundations subgraph — CI/CD still exists, just owned informally rather than by a required week-1 hire.
