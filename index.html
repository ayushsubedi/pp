Phase 1 Planning Notes

A growing collection of build-planning notes for RealEZ's Phase 1: two scenarios (scope, team, timeline, cost), a deep look at who we actually need to hire, job descriptions ready to post, and tech-stack reasoning. Both scenarios build with the same disciplined defaults — QA from month 1, foundations required in week 1 — as standard practice, not an option. This is a living reference — expect more pages over time.

Two scenarios, side by side

ScenarioShape
Full Pilot 6 months · 2–3 states · in-house ESIGN e-signature · direct earnest-money custody View →
Lean Pilot 5 months · 1 state · 3rd-party e-signature · no custody View →

Same team, same foundations-first discipline, same native mobile apps for all 5 portals in both — the real choice is product/legal scope and timeline, not process. See Scope for the full breakdown.
