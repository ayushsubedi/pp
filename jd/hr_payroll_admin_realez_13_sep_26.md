## About Husig & RealEZ

Husig (Human Signal Pvt. Ltd.) is an AI and data solutions consultancy based in Kathmandu, Nepal. This role supports **RealEZ**, a Husig product team building a full transaction OS for real estate, currently staffing up for its Phase 1 pilot build.

**Team:** RealEZ (Operations support) · **Reports to:** RealEZ's founder/CTO

## Why This Role Exists

Brought in week 1, alongside the tech lead, specifically to process onboarding, payroll, and paperwork for everyone else joining over the following weeks — a generalist operations role, not a senior HR specialist. Delaying this hire just means the founder or the tech lead does admin instead of their actual job.

## Key Responsibilities

### Onboarding & Payroll (50%)
- Process hiring paperwork for each new team member as they land
- Run monthly payroll and manage statutory compliance (PF/SSF) for the team

### Office & Admin (30%)
- Manage office setup, including hardware procurement and the office inverter/UPS
- Vendor and contractor invoicing

### HR Generalist Support (20%)
- Leave tracking, contracts, and offer letters as the team grows

## Must-Have

- 1–3 years in an HR/admin/office-management generalist role, ideally at a small company or startup
- Familiarity with Nepal payroll and statutory requirements (PF/SSF)
- Organized, comfortable being the first point of contact for a growing team
- Professional English

## Nice-to-Have

- Experience setting up HR/payroll processes from scratch at an early-stage company
- Basic bookkeeping familiarity

## First 90 Days

- **30 days:** onboarding process for the rest of the hiring plan running smoothly; payroll live
- **60 days:** office fully set up (hardware, inverter/UPS, admin processes)
- **90 days:** HR processes (leave, contracts) running independently
