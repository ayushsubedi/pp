## About Husig & RealEZ

Husig (Human Signal Pvt. Ltd.) is an AI and data solutions consultancy based in Kathmandu, Nepal. This role sits on the team building **RealEZ**, a Husig product: a full transaction OS for real estate, currently in its Phase 1 pilot build.

**Team:** RealEZ Engineering · **Reports to:** RealEZ's founder/CTO

## Why This Role Exists

Deliberately not a week-1 hire. This role joins in month 2, once there are 5+ builders and real cross-team dependency dates — a confirmed pilot state, a contracted e-signature vendor, a contracted pilot brokerage, all owned by cofounders outside engineering — that need active tracking. Earlier than that, a lean team is more efficient coordinating directly.

## Key Responsibilities

### Sprint & Delivery Coordination (40%)
- Run the team's sprint cadence; track the two product pillars against the roadmap
- Surface blockers early, especially where they trace back to an external dependency

### Cross-Team Dependency Tracking (35%)
- Own the dependency checklist with the GTM and Compliance cofounders (pilot state confirmation, e-signature vendor contract, pilot brokerage)
- Keep engineering unblocked by chasing these proactively, not reactively

### Reporting & Communication (25%)
- Status reporting to the founder
- Document decisions alongside the tech lead's architecture decision records

## Must-Have

- 2+ years PM experience in a software engineering context, ideally at an early-stage startup
- Comfortable running a lean process for a ~7-person team without over-processing it
- Strong written communication; comfortable tracking dependencies across internal and external (cofounder) stakeholders
- Professional English

## Nice-to-Have

- Experience in real estate, proptech, or another regulated vertical
- Experience working with a Nepal-based engineering team alongside a remote/international founder

## First 90 Days

- **30 days:** sprint cadence and dependency tracker running
- **60 days:** reporting rhythm established with the founder and cofounders
- **90 days:** coordinating the go/no-go readiness review for pilot launch
