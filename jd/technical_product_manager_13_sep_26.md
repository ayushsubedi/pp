**Start date:** available from Mangsir 1 (November 17, 2026)

## About Husig

Husig (Human Signal Pvt. Ltd.) is an AI and data solutions consultancy based in Kathmandu, Nepal. This role sits on the team building one of Husig's own products: a full transaction OS for real estate, currently in its Phase 1 pilot build.

**Team:** Product Engineering · **Reports to:** the founder (part-time)

## Why This Role Exists

A week-1 hire, not a month-2 addition. The founder is part-time for now and isn't driving vision, roadmap, or day-to-day prioritization — that can't just be dropped, and nobody else on the team is positioned to own it. This role owns product direction for the two pillars, runs delivery coordination, and tracks the cross-team dependencies owed by cofounders outside engineering (pilot state confirmation, e-signature vendor, pilot brokerage). "Technical" matters: you need enough fluency in how the product actually works to make real trade-off calls, not just track a board.

## Key Responsibilities

### Product Direction & Roadmap (35%)
- Own prioritization and roadmap sequencing for the two product pillars in the founder's absence from day-to-day decisions
- Make real scope trade-off calls informed by the product's actual technical shape, in partnership with the tech lead

### Sprint & Delivery Coordination (30%)
- Run the team's sprint cadence; track both pillars against the roadmap
- Surface blockers early, especially where they trace back to an external dependency

### Cross-Team Dependency Tracking (25%)
- Own the dependency checklist with the cofounders running go-to-market and compliance (pilot state confirmation, e-signature vendor contract, pilot brokerage)
- Keep engineering unblocked by chasing these proactively, not reactively

### Reporting & Communication (10%)
- Status reporting to the founder
- Document decisions alongside the tech lead's architecture decision records

## Must-Have

- 3+ years in product management or technical program/project management in a software engineering context, ideally at an early-stage startup
- Comfortable owning roadmap and prioritization calls, not just tracking a delivery board
- Technical enough to understand a data model and an integration constraint well enough to make a real trade-off call, without needing to write the code yourself
- Comfortable running a lean process for a ~7-person team without over-processing it
- Strong written communication; comfortable tracking dependencies across internal and external (cofounder) stakeholders
- Professional English

## Nice-to-Have

- Experience in real estate, proptech, or another regulated vertical
- Experience owning product direction with a founder who isn't full-time on day-to-day decisions
- Experience working with a Nepal-based engineering team alongside a remote/international founder

## First 90 Days

- **30 days:** roadmap and sprint cadence running; dependency tracker live
- **60 days:** making real prioritization calls independently, reporting rhythm established with the founder and cofounders
- **90 days:** coordinating the go/no-go readiness review for pilot launch

Send your CV plus a short note on a product/roadmap decision you owned end-to-end on a past project — the trade-off, and why you called it that way.
