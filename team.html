Who do we actually need

Team

The question isn't "how many people" — it's what actually creates risk in this specific build, and who removes that risk fastest. Neither a dedicated DevOps/Platform Engineer nor a second mobile engineer makes the cut, even with room for 8–10 people at no additional cost — both are judged genuinely covered at this team size, not cut for budget.

The one idea underneath all of this: AI coding assistance means a senior team covers more ground than the same size team could before. A tech lead with Claude, using well-reviewed Terraform/CI templates, can stand up and maintain solid infrastructure without a dedicated platform hire. One senior Flutter engineer, the same way, can cover Agent + Client native parity without a second. Budget headroom exists, but it doesn't get spent just because it's there — it stays open for a role that's actually justified when one comes up (see below).

The single highest-leverage hire on this list, and the one place seniority is worth a real premium (see Cost).

  • 5

7 hired, headroom left fully open

3 new FTE hires (tech lead, QA, PM) + HR/Admin = 4 new FTE, plus the 2 existing engineers, plus 1 project-based designer — 7 people total against a confirmed 8–10 budget headroom. That headroom stays unspent on purpose: neither DevOps/Platform nor a second mobile engineer earned a seat on their own merits, so the room goes unused rather than filled to hit a number. If a real need shows up — a second backend engineer for Scenario A's 2–3-state jurisdiction workload, a security engineer once there's production traffic to justify it — the room is there.

What's not hired, and why

  • A dedicated DevOps/Platform Engineer — the tech lead, with AI-assisted IaC/CI scaffolding, covers this at this team size. Not a budget call; revisit once there's real production traffic and on-call load to justify a dedicated owner.
  • A second mobile engineer — one senior Flutter engineer with AI assistance covers Agent + Client native parity. The tightest assumption in this plan, and worth watching, but the right default bet.
  • A Compliance & Trust Operations Lead — even in Scenario A's direct-custody path, compliance is owned by other cofounders, not staffed inside the build team. See the detailed earnest-money custody risk on Scenario A.
  • A dedicated "AI tooling" or prompt-engineering specialist — a practice (a written AI-usage policy, see Tech Stack), not a role, at this team size
  • A dedicated security engineer as an FTE — contract a pen test before pilot launch instead
  • Any GTM hire on the build side — owned by other cofounders in every scenario
